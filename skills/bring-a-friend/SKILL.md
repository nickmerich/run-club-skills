# Bring a Friend

The best invite is a member texting a friend: "Come run with us Saturday, I'll run with you." Make that easy and make it normal.

## Monthly bring-your-crew run

- Same ritual, same time. Not a separate event.
- Add an easy short route so invited friends can say yes.
- Every member who brings someone is that person's buddy.
- Welcome circle names guests and who brought them.

## Make inviting easy

Give members copy-paste text with the details as visible text, including the signup link written out:

> Running with [club] this [day] at [time] at [spot]. Easy pace, nobody gets left, coffee after. Run with me? [signup URL]

Also: a share card for stories, and a "bring someone next week" line in every recap.

## Attribution (light)

At check-in, ask first-timers one optional question: "Did someone invite you?" Record the name. That's all you need to know who your Bringers are.

## Thank publicly, ask privately

- Recap: "Thanks [Bringer] for bringing [Guest]" only with both people's OK.
- Privately: tell a Bringer when their friend comes back a second time.
- Never turn it into a points race or reward quantity over people.

## Measure

First-timers who were invited by a member, and how many of them came back within two weeks, versus first-timers who found you some other way.

# Routes and Safety

## Routes

- Start and end at the meeting spot and the hang.
- Out-and-back with turnarounds at each distance option means everyone finishes together. Loops work if each option returns to the spot.
- Offer an easy option every week (2 to 3 miles, walk/run welcome).
- Mark turnarounds and confusing junctions. Share a map link in every run post.
- Avoid unprotected busy crossings; if unavoidable, a leader stops the group and crosses together.

## Groups

- Pace groups by talking pace, each with a named leader.
- A sweep at the back of every group. The sweep is never the newest person.
- Regroup points on longer routes.
- Leaders carry a phone; someone has a basic first aid kit; know the nearest AED.

## Weather (decide thresholds in advance, one decision owner)

- **Lightning:** thunder heard or lightning seen means stop and get to a building or hard-topped vehicle; resume 30 minutes after the last thunder ([NWS](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-sports)).
- **Heat:** use WBGT or heat index, start earlier, shorten routes, set a cancel level. Unacclimatized beginners need more caution than published race thresholds.
- **Cold and ice:** run in cold with layers; move or cancel for ice on the route.
- **Air quality:** set an AQI level where you move indoors or cancel.
- Tell people where weather updates appear and when.

## Dark hours

Lights front and back, reflective layers, no headphones at crossings, buddy up, routes with lighting. Leaders count heads at the start and finish.

## Waivers, insurance, permits

- Have everyone sign a waiver (digital at RSVP, paper backup). Get it reviewed for your state.
- Liability insurance protects leaders and the club. Run club associations often offer club coverage; compare with a broker.
- Small free group runs on public paths often don't need a permit, but triggers include size, promotion, fees, sales, amplified sound, food or drinks, and road use. Ask the land owner in writing before you grow or promote.
- Water stations, cups, and giveaways on trails may be restricted. Ask.

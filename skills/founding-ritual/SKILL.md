# Founding Ritual

The clubs that last pick one rhythm and protect it. People plan their week around something that never moves.

## Lock six elements

| Element | Decide | Why |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Day | One day a week | Habit needs repetition |
| Time | Exact start time; arrival window before it | On time every time builds trust |
| Meeting spot | Exact spot with a map pin, parking, and a bathroom | First-timers need to find you |
| Routes | 2 to 4 signature routes, out-and-back or loops to the same spot | Everyone finishes together |
| Post-run hang | Coffee or a gathering spot, 20 to 30 minutes minimum | The hang is where the club happens |
| Frequency | Every week | Gaps break the habit |

Pick the hang before the route. Then pick routes that end there.

## The promise

One sentence, repeated everywhere (bio, Strava, site, every welcome):

> [Club] runs every [day] at [time] from [spot]. All paces. Nobody gets left behind.

## Drift audit (monthly)

| Signal | Means | Fix |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Start time slipping | Convenience creeping in | Start on time, every time |
| Leader late or missing | Weak signal | Backup captain (see `captains-and-succession`) |
| Hang skipped | Retention will drop next | Restore it, even with three people |
| Route changes every week with no theme | Novelty over identity | Rotate signature routes |
| Promo replacing the run post | Ritual becoming an ad channel | Lead with the run and the people |

## Weather and cancellations

Run in rain and cold when it's safe. Cancel or delay only against **written thresholds** decided in advance with one decision owner (see `routes-and-safety`): lightning, dangerous heat, ice, severe air quality, official advisories.

If you cancel or move it:
1. Post as early as possible, at the time you've told people to check.
2. Say exactly why.
3. Offer a make-up or a solo route people can do and share.
4. Next week's post: "We're back," and restore the ritual exactly.

Never cancel silently. Never cancel for low RSVPs.

## Output

Ritual lock sheet (the six elements + promise), drift audit (green/yellow/red per element with one fix each), or a cancellation message and recovery post.

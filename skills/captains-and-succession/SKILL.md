# Captains and Succession

## Roles

| Role | Does | Minimum per run |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Lead | Starts on time, welcome circle, route, safety calls | 1 |
| Greeter | First 15 seconds with newcomers | 1 (can be the lead for small groups) |
| Buddy | Runs with a first-timer | 1 per first-timer |
| Sweep | Last person on the route | 1 per group |
| Check-in | QR or list | 1 |
| Recap | Photos and recap draft | 1 |

## Picking captains

Look for regulars who show up early, talk to new people without being asked, and stay for the hang. Ask personally. Start them as sweep or greeter, then lead with you, then lead alone.

Aim for one backup lead within the first three months and two within six.

## Handoff checklist (when the founder is away)

- Route and map link
- Weather thresholds and who decides
- Check-in method
- Expected first-timers and their buddies
- Emergency contacts and nearest AED
- What to post and when

## Founder time budget

Write down the hours you have each week. Protect the things only you should do (personal follow-ups to first-timers early on, partner relationships), delegate the rest, and drop anything that doesn't improve the run.

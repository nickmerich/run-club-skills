# Run Reports and Stories

## Weekly recap

1. One real moment from the run (specific, with permission if it names someone).
2. The numbers from check-ins: people, first-timers, milestones. Label anything that isn't from a record.
3. Thanks: buddies, sweeps, partners.
4. Next run: day, time, spot, route, the signup URL written out.

Plain voice. No hype words, no invented quotes, no emojis unless that's genuinely your club's style.

## Member stories

- Ask first. Explain where it'll appear.
- A 15-minute call with four questions: what got you running, a hard thing you did, what the club changed, what you'd tell someone nervous about coming.
- Write it, send it back to them for approval, publish only after a yes.
- Health details, kids, and workplace stay out unless they explicitly want them in.
- One story can feed a post, a caption set, a recap lead, and a welcome-message reference.

## Photos

Real photos of your people beat graphics. Respect anyone who opts out. Put first-timers in the middle only if they're happy to be in it.

# Drifter Save

## Who's a drifter

Two or more prior check-ins, then three or more runs missed in a row, with no known reason. Confirm the check-in data is complete first.

## Diagnose before writing

| Likely reason | Signals | Play | Avoid |
|---------------|---------|------|-------|
| Schedule | Still active elsewhere, just not on run day | Acknowledge, offer a solo route or another day | "Hope to see you this week" |
| Injury / health | Activity dropped or changed, or they mentioned it | Care message, no ask. Move to Paused. | Any invite |
| Life event | New baby, move, new job, loss | Care only. Paused. Check back much later. | Club asks |
| Lost connection | Active but quiet with the club | Message from the person they ran with most, not the founder | Founder form letter |
| Quiet quit | Unfollowed, no reply to two messages | One last kind note, then Released | More messages |

When unsure between two reasons, pick the more generous one.

## Message rules

- Name a specific shared memory.
- No guilt words: "haven't seen you," "where've you been," "you should."
- No ask in the first message. The invite comes only if they reply.
- Three sentences max.
- Right sender: founder for anchors and long-timers; their buddy or running friend for most regulars.

> [Name], still think about you dragging the whole group up [hill] in August. Hope the new job's treating you well. No agenda, just thinking of you.

## Stop rule

Two unanswered messages over about four weeks → Released. One short note honoring their time, and stop. Keeping people in limbo wastes effort and feels like pressure.

## Track

Saves (came back within 30 days), replies, and moves to Paused or Released. Report the count, not the names, outside the leadership group.

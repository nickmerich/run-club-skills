# Local Partners

## Fit test

A partner is a fit if they make the run better for members: a place to hang, gear help, recovery, product people would actually use. If the only value is a logo, skip it.

## Start with the hang

The post-run spot is the most important partner. Ask for something small and real: a table on run day, a heads-up to staff, a member perk. Give them a predictable group of customers every week.

## The ask

Specific, useful, and small first:
- Coffee shop: "Can we make you our post-run spot every [day]? We'll bring [verified range] people."
- Run store: shoe-fit night, a monthly group run from the store, a member perk.
- Brand: product for first-timers, a monthly run presenting partner.

Use honest numbers: weekly check-in range, unique runners, months running. Never follower counts as attendance.

## Small partner menu (examples to adapt)

| Option | Partner gets | Club gets |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Hang partner | Weekly group of customers, thanks in recaps | Table, perk |
| Monthly run host | Run from their location once a month | Product, perk |
| Presenting partner | Name on the weekly run post | Cash or product to fund the club |

## After each partner moment

Send a short recap: what happened, verified count, photos (with consent), and one idea for next month.

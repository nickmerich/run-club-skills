# Buddy System

## Who qualifies

Regulars who have come three or more weeks in a row, are friendly, and are willing to run someone else's pace for a day. Ask them personally. No application.

The ask:
> [Name], you've been here three weeks straight and people like running with you. Want to buddy a first-timer now and then? You'd just run their pace and introduce them around. No pressure.

## The buddy brief (one page)

- One newcomer per buddy per run.
- Run their pace, finish with them. No workouts that day.
- Ask what got them running and where they live.
- Introduce them to at least two people.
- Walk them to the hang.
- Tell the leader one thing about them for the follow-up.

## Weekly pairing

Inputs: expected first-timers (RSVPs, DMs, friends of members) and available buddies.

Match on, in order: pace, who invited them, something in common (neighborhood, kids, past sport), and keeping buddies from repeating every single week.

Output:
```
[Newcomer] → [Buddy] (why: [pace match / same neighborhood])
Walk-up backup buddies: [names]
```

## Pool health

- Expected first-timers > available buddies: tap two more regulars this week.
- A buddy who did three weeks straight: give them a week off.
- Track pairings so recognition is accurate.

## Recognition

Thank buddies by name in the recap (with permission), a small perk at a milestone, and tell them when someone they buddied comes back. That last one matters most.

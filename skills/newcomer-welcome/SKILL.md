# Newcomer Welcome

First-timers decide in the first few minutes whether they belong. Three things make the difference: a real welcome, nobody running alone, and a personal follow-up.

## On arrival (the first 15 seconds)

1. Eye contact, walk over. Don't wave from across the lot.
2. "Hey, first time with us?" Let them answer.
3. "I'm [name]. What's yours?" Say their name back.
4. One-line expectations: "We do [routes], easy pace, nobody gets left, and we hang at [spot] after."
5. **Introduce their buddy by name.** Never skip this step.

## Pre-run circle

"Any first-timers?" Say each name out loud. "Glad you're here." Short. That moment matters more than any speech.

## During the run

The buddy matches the newcomer's pace, asks what got them running, and introduces them to at least two other people.

## The hang

The buddy walks them to the hang. A leader stops by with one specific comment from the run. Newcomers stand in the middle of the group photo, not the edge (only if they're OK being photographed). Before they leave: "Same time next week?"

## Follow-ups (personal, never a blast)

**Within 24 hours.** Five ingredients:
1. Their name
2. One specific moment from the run
3. Their buddy's name
4. One concrete reason to come back next week
5. A question they can answer in one tap

> Hey [Name], [leader] from [club]. Loved how you kept going on that last climb. Glad you and [buddy] ran together. Next week we're doing [route], good one for week two. You in?

**Day 3 (optional):** a useful link or a midweek run if the club has one.
**Day before the next run:** "Tomorrow [time], [spot]. Bring someone if you want."

Stop after that. If they come back, they're in the regular flow. If not, they're a quiet first-timer, not a drifter.

## Monthly audit

| Check | Target |
|-------|--------|
| First-timers greeted by name | All |
| First-timers with a buddy | All |
| First-timers followed up within 24 hours | All |
| First-timers who returned within 14 days | Set your own target from your own data; track the trend |

A miss on any "All" row means fix the process this week before trying to get more first-timers.

## Voice

Casual, specific, short. No guilt, no hype, no form-letter phrases. Send from a real person.
